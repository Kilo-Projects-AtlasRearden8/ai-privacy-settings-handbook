Deleting your history

Dated: October 27, 2026

Want to clear out old AI conversations? This section shows how to delete your chat history, app by app, and what "deleted" really means.

The short version

  • You can usually delete individual chats or your entire history from the app's settings.
  • Deleted chats are typically removed from your view right away, but the company may keep them for a short grace period (often ~30 days) for safety and abuse review.
  • Deleting your history does not undo any training that already happened on those chats.

ChatGPT (OpenAI)

Delete one chat:

  1. Open ChatGPT.
  2. Find the chat in your sidebar history.
  3. Click the three dots (•••) next to the chat → Delete.

Delete all chats:

  1. Click your profile picture → Settings → Data controls.
  2. Choose Clear all chats (or "Delete all") and confirm.

Official help: OpenAI - Delete your ChatGPT account or data

Claude (Anthropic)

Delete one chat:

  1. Open the chat in claude.ai.
  2. Click the three dots (•••) or the trash icon next to the chat → Delete.

Delete all chats:

  1. Click your profile picture → Settings.
  2. Look for Clear conversation history or Delete all chats and confirm.

Official help: Anthropic - Claude data handling

Gemini (Google)

Delete activity:

  1. Go to myactivity.google.com or open Gemini Apps Activity from gemini.ai.
  2. Filter by Gemini.
  3. Choose Delete → pick a date range (last hour, last day, custom range, or all time).

You can also set Auto-delete to remove Gemini activity automatically after 3, 18, or 36 months.

Official help: Google - Delete Gemini Apps Activity

Microsoft Copilot

  1. Go to account.microsoft.com/privacy and sign in.
  2. Find Copilot history or Copilot activity history.
  3. Choose what to delete and confirm.

For work or school accounts (Microsoft 365 Copilot), your IT admin manages retention — ask them before deleting anything you might need.

Official help: Microsoft - Manage your Copilot data

Perplexity

  1. Log in at perplexity.ai.
  2. Open your Library (your past threads).
  3. Click the three dots on a thread → Delete.
  4. For bulk deletion, check Account settings → Data & privacy for a "Clear history" option.

Official help: Perplexity - Privacy center

Meta AI

Meta AI chats live inside Messenger, Instagram, and WhatsApp.

  • Open the conversation with Meta AI.
  • Swipe left (mobile) or use the conversation menu → Delete conversation.

Deleting the chat removes it from your inbox; Meta's retention of the underlying data follows Meta's data policy.

Official help: Meta - Generative AI privacy

Grok (xAI)

  1. In X, open your Grok conversation history.
  2. Use the delete/remove option on individual conversations.
  3. Check X Settings → Privacy for account-level data controls.

Official help: xAI - Privacy policy

Local AI (Ollama, LM Studio, etc.)

There is no cloud account. Your chats are files on your computer:

  • Ollama: chat data lives in Ollama's local database on your machine. To delete, clear chats inside the app or remove the Ollama data folder.
  • LM Studio: conversations are stored locally in the app's data folder.

Delete the files or use the app's "Clear chat" button. Once deleted from your device, they're gone (unless you have a backup).

What "deleted" really means

What happensTypical timing
Chat disappears from your appImmediately
Chat removed from company's active systemsUsually within ~30 days
Chat kept for safety/abuse reviewUp to ~30 days, sometimes longer
Already-used for model trainingNot undone

Tips

  • Delete regularly if you don't want old chats lingering.
  • Turn on auto-delete where available (Gemini, Google) so you don't have to remember.
  • Before deleting anything for work, check if your employer needs those records.
  • Sensitive chat already in an AI? Delete it, then assume the company may still have a copy for a short window.
