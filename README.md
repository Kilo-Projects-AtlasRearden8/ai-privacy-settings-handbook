# AI Privacy Settings Handbook

A beginner-friendly manual for the privacy and data-training settings in popular AI apps and assistants. Plain English, step by step, dated, with official help links and a printable checklist.

Written like a friend would explain it — never condescending, never assuming you already know.

## What's inside

- [`docs/`](docs/) — the manual in Markdown (easy to print or copy into your own notes)
- [`static/`](static/) — a small static website (HTML/CSS, no build step, open any file in your browser)
- [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) — MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Asdale Dynamics, LLC

## Who this is for

Anyone who uses AI apps (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, Meta AI, Grok, and more) and wants to know:

- What gets stored about your chats
- How to opt out of having your conversations used to train models
- How to delete your history
- What is and isn't safe to share
- Workplace and health-data cautions

No technical background needed.
