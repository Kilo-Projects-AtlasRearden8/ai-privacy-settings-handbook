# AI Privacy Settings Handbook

A beginner-friendly manual for the privacy and data-training settings in popular AI apps and assistants. Plain English, step by step, dated October 27, 2026, with official help links and a printable checklist.

Written like a friend would explain it — never condescending, never assuming you already know.

## What's inside

- [`docs/`](docs/) — the manual in Markdown (easy to print or copy into your own notes)
  - [00-intro.md](docs/00-intro.md) — how to use this guide
  - [01-what-gets-stored.md](docs/01-what-gets-stored.md) — what AI apps keep about your chats
  - [02-opt-out-training.md](docs/02-opt-out-training.md) — stop your chats being used to train models
  - [03-deleting-history.md](docs/03-deleting-history.md) — erase past conversations

## Who this is for

Anyone who uses AI apps — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, Meta AI, Grok, and more — and wants to know, in plain English:

- What gets stored about your chats
- How to opt out of having your conversations used to train models
- How to delete your history
- What is and isn't safe to share
- Workplace and health-data cautions

No technical background needed.
