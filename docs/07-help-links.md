## How to find help pages yourself

Most companies keep privacy settings under:

- **Settings** → **Privacy** or **Data controls** inside the app
- **Account** → **Privacy dashboard** on the company website
- Search: `"[App name] privacy settings opt out training"`

## A note on links

Links were checked as of **October 27, 2026**. If a link doesn't work, go to the company's main website and look for "Help," "Support," or "Privacy."
