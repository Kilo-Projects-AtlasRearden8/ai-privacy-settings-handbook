# What gets stored

**Dated October 27, 2026**

When you chat with an AI, the conversation doesn't just vanish when you close the tab. Most AI apps keep some record of what you type and what the AI replies. Here is what typically gets stored, app by app, in plain English.

## The short version

Almost every AI app stores:

- **Your messages** (the prompts you type)
- **The AI's replies**
- **When the chat happened** (date and time)
- **Your account info** (email, name, device, IP address)

Many apps also keep this data even after you "delete" a chat, for a limited time, for safety and abuse-prevention reasons. How long varies by company.

## App-by-app breakdown

| App | What gets stored | Where it's stored | How long (typical) |
|-----|------------------|-------------------|--------------------|
| **ChatGPT** (OpenAI) | Your prompts, OpenAI's replies, your account info, device and IP data | OpenAI's servers | Deleted chats are removed from OpenAI's systems within about 30 days (may be kept longer for abuse review) |
| **Claude** (Anthropic) | Your prompts, Claude's replies, account info | Anthropic's servers | Retained per Anthropic's retention schedule; deleted chats are flagged for deletion |
| **Gemini** (Google) | Your prompts, Gemini's replies, linked Google account activity | Google's servers | Depends on your Google account auto-delete settings (default can be 18 months) |
| **Microsoft Copilot** | Your prompts, Copilot's replies, your Microsoft account activity | Microsoft's servers | Governed by your Microsoft account privacy settings |
| **Perplexity** | Your prompts, Perplexity's replies, search history tied to your account | Perplexity's servers | Retained per Perplexity's privacy policy |
| **Meta AI** | Your prompts, Meta AI's replies, activity across Meta products | Meta's servers | Per Meta's data policy |
| **Grok** (xAI) | Your prompts, Grok's replies, X account activity if linked | xAI's servers | Per xAI's privacy policy |
| **Local AI** (Ollama, LM Studio, etc.) | Nothing leaves your computer unless you choose to sync | Your own device | As long as you keep the files |

## What this means for you

- **Your chats are not private in the way a diary is.** The company running the AI can see them, at least while they're stored.
- **Employees or contractors may review chats.** Many AI companies use human reviewers to look at a small sample of conversations to check for safety issues or improve the model.
- **Deleted does not always mean instantly gone.** There is often a grace period (commonly around 30 days) before deleted chats are fully removed from the company's systems.

## Tips

- If you wouldn't want a stranger reading it, don't paste it into an AI chat.
- Use temporary or "incognito" chats when available (see the opt-out section).
- Check your account settings for auto-delete options so old chats don't linger for months.
