# AI Privacy Settings Handbook

**A beginner's guide to what AI apps remember, how to opt out of training, and how to delete your history.**

Plain English, step by step, dated October 27, 2026. Published by Asdale Dynamics, LLC.

---

## Welcome

If you've ever wondered "Does ChatGPT remember what I type?" or "Is my boss able to see my AI chats?", this guide is for you.

We walk through the most popular AI apps and assistants — what they store, how to keep your conversations out of their training data, how to delete your history, and what you should never paste into an AI chat. No technical background required. Written like a friend would explain it: clear, warm, and never condescending.

## How to use this guide

Each section covers one topic. You can read it start to finish, or jump straight to the app you use:

- [What gets stored](01-what-gets-stored.md) — the basics of what AI apps keep
- [Opting out of training](02-opt-out-training.md) — app-by-app steps to stop your chats being used to train models
- [Deleting your history](03-deleting-history.md) — how to erase past conversations
- [Safe sharing](04-safe-sharing.md) — what is and isn't okay to paste into an AI
- [Workplace and health-data cautions](05-workplace-health-cautions.md) — extra care for work and medical information
- [Printable checklist](06-printable-checklist.md) — a one-page checklist to print and keep by your desk
- [Official help links](07-help-links.md) — direct links to each company's privacy help pages

## A quick reminder before we start

AI settings change. Companies update their apps and their privacy policies all the time. The steps here were accurate as of **October 27, 2026**, but always double-check the official help links if something looks different on your screen.

This guide is general education, not legal or medical advice. When in doubt about sensitive data, ask your workplace's IT or security team — or keep the data out of the AI entirely.
